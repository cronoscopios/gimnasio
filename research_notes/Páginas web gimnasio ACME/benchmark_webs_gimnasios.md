# Benchmark de estructura web (páginas y navegación) de gimnasios líderes en España — para Gimnasio ACME (gimnasioacme.com)

Method note: In October 2026 each gym's live homepage was fetched (header, mega-menu and footer were extracted). The sites checked were Basic-Fit (mcfit.com/es now 308-redirects to Basic-Fit), VivaGym, Altafit, GO fit, DiR, Metropolitan, Anytime Fitness Spain and Wezone (a CrossFit/boutique group). Synergym returned an empty, JS-rendered page and Holmes Place could not be reached (404 / connection refused), so neither has data. No sitemap.xml files were fetched, so the results cover visible navigation only. Agency and software articles were also used. They are vendor content and possibly biased.

## 1. What pages and top-level nav items do large chains in Spain have?

### Takeaway
Every chain's header follows the same pattern: a club finder, prices/membership plans ("Precios"/"Cuotas"/"Tarifas"), classes/activities, facilities/services, a blog, a member-area link and one prominent sign-up CTA ("Apúntate"/"Inscríbete"/"Únete al club"/"Empezar"). The footer carries customer service/FAQ, careers, the app, social links and a dense legal block.

### Inferences

- Prices are labelled "Precios" (Basic-Fit), "Cuotas" (VivaGym), "Centros y Tarifas" / "Planes" (GO fit). In chains, prices often sit inside the club page because they vary by location.
- Classes are labelled "Clases colectivas", "Clases", "Actividades", "Actividades y Cursos" or "Fitness". "Horarios" is a top-level item at GO fit and DiR.

## 2. Boutique gyms, independent single-location gyms and CrossFit boxes in Spain

### Takeaway
The one boutique/CrossFit example analysed in depth (Wezone, a multi-site box brand) uses a simpler, activity-centric navigation: Centros, Actividades (one page per discipline), Fisioterapia, Nutrición, APP, Sobre nosotros, Blog. Its main conversion tool is a "PRUEBA GRATIS" free trial class, and it publishes no prices. Data on truly single-location independents is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Wezone nav: "Centros" (Madrid/Barcelona), "Actividades" (Pulse Method; CrossFit with Fundamentals, Crossfit, Weightlifting, Gymnastics, Open Box; Hyrox; Fitness with Pillars, Core, Mobility), "Fisioterapia", "Nutrición", "APP", "Sobre nosotros" (Throwdown, challenges, "Wezone B2B", RSC, "Qué es Crossfit", "Plan de Carrera", "Únete al equipo"), "Blog". Languages ES/EN/CA. [Source](https://www.wezone.es/)
- Wezone footer: Centros, Actividades, Sobre nosotros, Contacto, Legal (Política de privacidad, Política de cookies, Aviso legal), social (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube), app downloads and a newsletter form. [Source](https://www.wezone.es/)
- Wezone's "PRUEBA GRATIS" leads to a dedicated subdomain with a landing page per centre (pruebagratis.wezone.es/prueba/almagro). There is no prices page. Coaches appear as a homepage section rather than a page. The in-house "Wezone App" handles class booking, chat and progress tracking. [Source](https://www.wezone.es/)
- CrossFit.com's listing for CrossFit Wezone Chamberí shows the schedule as an image and says "Class times are subject to change, contact this gym to verify". [Source](https://crossfit.com/gym/25543/crossfit-wezone-chamberi)
- Time Out says UrbanBox CrossFit (Embajadores) offers a trial session and "flexible" rates, with contact through its website or email. [Source](https://www.timeout.es/madrid/es/deportes/urban-box-embajadores)
- Small boxes and studios also take bookings through aggregators: CrossFit db asks first-timers to book by WhatsApp and arrive 15 minutes early ([Source](https://urbansportsclub.com/es/venues/crossfit-db)), and CrossFit Montecarmelo takes bookings by phone ([Source](https://urbansportsclub.com/es/venues/crossfit-montecarmelo)). Barcelona boutiques (She Barre, Casa Barré, B3B) are listed on Wellhub ([Source](https://wellhub.com/es-es/search/partners/casa-barre-barcelona-barcelona/)).

### Inferences
- Boutiques and boxes usually split activities into one page per discipline, which also serves SEO. They show coaches prominently and push a free trial class through a lead form or landing page rather than a checkout.
- Many small gyms rely on WhatsApp, phone or aggregators (Urban Sports Club, Wellhub) for first contact. A "Partners / aceptamos Urban Sports Club / Wellhub" mention could be relevant.

### Gaps
- No genuinely single-location independent gym in Spain was crawled because the call budget ran out. Further examples are needed, such as a local "gimnasio de barrio" and a single-site CrossFit box (for example urbanbox, crossfit db).
- Third-party booking software (AimHarder, Resawod, Virtuagym, Trainingym) was not confirmed on any examined site.

## 4. Sign-up, class schedules and member area

### Takeaway
Low-cost chains use full online checkout (Basic-Fit /checkout, VivaGym /joining, GO fit /alta, DiR alta.dir.cat). Premium clubs and boxes lean on "Únete" or trial lead forms. Most chains run their own branded app for booking and the member area, with a web login as a secondary channel.

### Cited Findings
- Basic-Fit: online checkout at /es-es/checkout. A day pass is available. The member area is My Basic-Fit (web) plus the Basic-Fit app. [Source](https://www.basic-fit.com/es-es/home)
- VivaGym: "Apúntate" leads to the joining flow with plan choice (ONE / FLEX / PRIME / PASE DE 1 DÍA). Payment icons are shown. There is also a free 1-day invitation form, plus the VivaGym MyApp and an "Área de cliente" on the web. [Source](https://www.vivagym.com/es-es/)
- GO fit: online sign-up at /alta, which has an incentive (the TGS key free online versus 10 € at the centre), plus a callback lead form for help or a trial. Schedules are published at /horarios. The app is My GOfit. [Source](https://www.go-fit.es/)
- DiR: "APÚNTATE" leads to the subdomain alta.dir.cat. Schedules are at /es/horarios. The member area "Conectarse" is a legacy .aspx web portal. [Source](https://www.dir.cat/es/)
- Altafit: "Inscríbete" leads to a club search, then sign-up. The member area is "My Altafit" on an external platform (provis.es). [Source](https://www.altafitgymclub.com/)
- Wezone: the free trial class goes through a landing subdomain per centre. Booking runs through the in-house Wezone App. [Source](https://www.wezone.es/)
- Anytime: the main CTA is "Pruébanos gratis" (try-us-free page). The AF App is promoted. [Source](https://www.anytimefitness.com/es-es)

### Inferences
- For a single gym, a realistic pattern is a primary CTA for online sign-up (checkout with plan selection) plus a secondary "Prueba gratis / pase de un día" lead form. The schedule should be a dedicated "Horarios" page, embedded from the booking software or the gym's own system. The member area should be a "Área de socio" link to the app or web portal.
- Separate sign-up subdomains (alta.dir.cat, pruebagratis.wezone.es) indicate third-party or separate systems. A single site could keep these flows on the main domain, for example /apuntate and /prueba-gratis.

### Gaps
- None of the checkout flows were walked through (steps, required data, SEPA vs card), so it is not confirmed whether VivaGym or DiR take full payment online.

## 5. Footer contents

### Takeaway
A Spanish gym footer typically has 3–5 link columns (services/info, about the company, legal), social icons, app badges and contact details (phone, sometimes WhatsApp or email). The legal block always includes Aviso legal, Política de privacidad and Política de cookies. Larger companies add contract/withdrawal terms, club rules, a canal ético and promotion terms.

### Cited Findings
- Basic-Fit's legal block adds "Derecho de rescisión de 14 días", "Reglamento de videovigilancia", "Reglamento interno" and T&C as a PDF. [Source](https://www.basic-fit.com/es-es/home)
- VivaGym's legal block adds "Normativa de los clubes", "Contrato y desistimiento", "Canal Ético" and "Condiciones de la promoción". [Source](https://www.vivagym.com/es-es/)
- GO fit uses a single "Políticas en línea" page with anchors, and shows phone, email, WhatsApp and support hours in the footer. [Source](https://www.go-fit.es/)
- DiR adds "Condiciones y formas de pago" and its phone number. [Source](https://www.dir.cat/es/)
- Wezone (boutique) keeps only the three basic legal pages, contact, a newsletter and app badges. [Source](https://www.wezone.es/)
- Social networks seen: Instagram is on 8/8. Facebook is on most. TikTok appears at Basic-Fit, VivaGym and DiR, YouTube at GO fit, DiR, Metropolitan, Anytime and Wezone, and LinkedIn at GO fit, DiR and Anytime. [Sources above]

### Inferences
- Chain footers rarely show an address or opening hours because they have many clubs. For a single-location gym, the footer should show the address, a map link, opening hours, phone/WhatsApp and email, the NAP block that matters for local SEO.
- Canal ético is legally relevant mainly for companies with 50 or more employees under Spain's Ley 2/2023. This is an inference from law that was not verified in this research, and it is probably not needed for a small gym.

### Gaps
- Spanish legal requirements were not verified in this research. The LSSI requires an aviso legal, and the RGPD/LOPDGDD requires privacy and cookie notices. The 14-day withdrawal right for online contracts and the hojas de reclamaciones notice need confirmation by legal research.

## 6. Chain vs single-location: what is relevant for Gimnasio ACME

### Takeaway
Much of a chain's information architecture is multi-location overhead: club finder, city SEO pages, per-club pricing, franchise and investor pages. A single gym can flatten this. The club page effectively becomes the whole site, with prices, schedules, facilities, location and hours at the top level and a single sign-up flow.

### Cited Findings
- Multi-location elements observed: "Busca un club" and city pages (Basic-Fit), "Gimnasios" (VivaGym), "GYM CLUBS" by province (Altafit), "Centros y Tarifas" (GO fit), a centre list and "Próximas aperturas" (DiR), clubs by city (Metropolitan), "Encuentra tu gimnasio" and "Abre tu franquicia" (Anytime), "Inversiones" and "Franquicias" (DiR). [Basic-Fit](https://www.basic-fit.com/es-es/home); [VivaGym](https://www.vivagym.com/es-es/); [Altafit](https://www.altafitgymclub.com/); [GO fit](https://www.go-fit.es/); [DiR](https://www.dir.cat/es/); [Anytime](https://www.anytimefitness.com/es-es)
- Pages also relevant to a single gym (seen at chains): opening hours page (Basic-Fit "Horarios de apertura"), day pass, membership freeze ("Congela la suscripción"), referral ("Invita a un amigo"), club rules ("Normativa"), personal training, physiotherapy, locker rental (VivaGym "Alquiler de taquillas"), gift ("Regala DiR"), corporate ("Fitness corporativo", "DiR para empresas"). [Sources above]

### Inferences (proposed for gimnasioacme.com; for the report writer to validate)
- **Essential:** Inicio; Tarifas (or "Precios"/"Cuotas"); Clases (one subpage per activity is optional) plus Horarios; Instalaciones; Entrenadores/Equipo; Apúntate (online sign-up); Prueba gratis / Pase de un día; Contacto y cómo llegar (map, hours, phone/WhatsApp); Preguntas frecuentes; Área de socio (link to the app or portal); legal pages (Aviso legal, Política de privacidad, Política de cookies, Condiciones de contratación / desistimiento, Normativa del gimnasio).
- **Optional:** Entrenamiento personal; Fisioterapia/Nutrición (if offered); Blog; Sobre nosotros; Trabaja con nosotros (a simple page or email suffices); Empresas (corporate agreements); Tarjeta regalo; Invita a un amigo; Testimonios (as a homepage section); Tienda.
- **Not needed:** club finder, city pages, franchise or investor pages, canal ético (unless the gym is above the legal threshold), sala de prensa.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence from independent single-location Spanish gyms was gathered to confirm this simplified structure empirically. Recommendations rest on chain patterns, one boutique group and vendor guidance.
